# Image brief: JSJ INFOTECH website (v2)

## Who the client is
JSJ INFOTECH is a technology and engineering company in Hyderabad, India. It works across:
enterprise IT and cloud, SAP projects (with partner SGN Software, an SAP Gold Partner), AI,
survey and inspection drones, custom software, maintenance of HVAC (air-conditioning) systems in
government buildings, and new-technology R&D. Buyers are Indian government departments and large
enterprises. The site must feel premium, serious, real and Indian, not generic stock.

## The problem with the last set
Images did not show what the text says. Example: text said "Cloud and ERP" over a plain city photo;
"2 anomalies flagged" over a drone with no visible inspection. Every image below MUST visibly show the
exact thing in its caption, so a viewer understands the message from the image alone.

## Global style rules (all images)
- Photorealistic, cinematic, premium corporate photography, natural light, high detail, sharp.
- Colour grade: deep navy and cool blue with subtle violet; small warm orange highlights allowed.
- Indian context where people or places appear (Indian architecture, Indian professionals).
- No readable text, no numbers, no brand logos anywhere in the image. UI on screens = abstract shapes,
  charts, maps, wireframes only.
- People: seen from behind, side or at a distance; no close-up faces.
- Composition notes are binding (where empty space goes, what sits where).
- Size: 16:9 landscape, at least 1600 px wide.

## Images (save each with the exact filename in the current directory)

1. hero-connected.png  (hero slide 1: "Intelligent Technology. Connected Enterprise.")
   Wide dusk aerial of an Indian city edge. Visible, distinct objects, each clearly readable:
   a glass corporate headquarters tower (centre-right), a factory with chimneys and warehouses (right),
   a government office building with rooftop HVAC units (far right), a solar farm (bottom right),
   and a data centre building (centre). Thin glowing light-lines (violet and teal) run from every
   building into the data centre, like a network map drawn over the landscape. LEFT 40% of the frame
   is calm dark sky and soft city lights only (headline text goes there).

2. hero-partnership.png  (hero slide 2: "Enterprise SAP delivery, powered by a joint partnership")
   Modern Indian enterprise workshop room. Two teams of consultants (4-5 people, from behind/side)
   around a large wall screen showing an abstract business process map: connected boxes for purchase,
   inventory, finance and HR flowing left to right, with one box being drawn by a hand. Laptops and
   a printed architecture diagram on the table. Warm daylight. Feels like a real joint project kickoff.
   RIGHT 35% slightly defocused (a logo card will overlay there); LEFT 40% darker wall (text goes there).

3. hero-drone-twin.png  (hero slide 3: drone survey, digital twin, anomalies flagged)
   A professional quadcopter drone (centre-right, clearly visible) hovering above a factory roof.
   From the drone, a thin blue scanning light grid is projected onto the roof surface. Two roof spots
   are clearly highlighted with glowing orange-red rings (damaged panels / water pooling).
   In the lower right, an engineer's hands hold a tablet showing a 3D wireframe model of the SAME
   building with the same two spots marked orange. LEFT 40% is calm sky (text goes there).

4. hero-ecosystem-bg.png  (hero slide 4 background, a diagram card goes on top)
   Very soft, defocused aerial of a modern Indian tech campus at blue hour, mostly bokeh and light,
   low contrast, so a white card on top stays readable.

5. physical-digital.png  (About section: "connection between physical and digital operations")
   One image split down the middle: LEFT half is a real Indian government plant room with large
   chillers, pipes and an engineer from behind with a tablet; the RIGHT half shows the SAME room as a
   glowing blue-violet digital-twin wireframe with small sensor dots on each machine. The two halves
   blend seamlessly at the centre line.

6. v-it.png  (IT Projects and Consulting: cloud and hybrid infrastructure, cybersecurity, AIOps)
   Engineers (from behind) in a bright Indian enterprise operations room. Big wall screen shows an

7. v-sap.png  (SAP Solutions with SGN: implementation, migration, support)
   Indian SAP consultants (from behind/side) with a client finance team in a meeting room. Screens show

8. v-drones.png  (Drone Technology: survey, mapping, inspection)
   Drone pilot in safety vest (from behind) at an Indian construction or infrastructure site, holding a

9. v-software.png  (Software Development: web, mobile and enterprise apps, APIs)
   Indian developers (from behind) in a bright studio. One large monitor shows a web dashboard design,

10. v-hvac.png  (HVAC Government Projects: AMC maintenance, IoT sensors, predictive maintenance)
    Technician in helmet and vest (from behind) on the rooftop of an Indian government building beside

    shows an abstract dashboard with one unit highlighted orange (predictive maintenance alert).

11. v-ai.png  (AI Innovations: computer vision, predictive models, copilots)
